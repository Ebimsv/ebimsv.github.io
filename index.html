About

Engineer first, with a research record

I am a Generative AI engineer and researcher based in Tehran. My work covers domain-specific LLMs: supervised fine-tuning, LoRA/QLoRA, preference alignment, RAG pipelines, and agent systems, plus generative vision models in production.

I lead applied R&D on industry collaborations, teach machine learning, and mentor students in deep learning and LLM research. I am looking for industry roles and Ph.D. opportunities in NLP, multimodal learning, and efficient model training.
