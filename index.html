About Me

A Brief Introduction

I am an AI engineer and researcher with a focus on natural language processing, machine learning, computer vision, and large language models. My work spans domain-specific LLM development—including supervised fine-tuning, LoRA/QLoRA, preference alignment, RAG pipelines, and agent systems—as well as generative vision models for real-world deployment.

Based in Tehran, Iran, I lead applied R&D across industry collaborations while teaching machine learning and mentoring students on deep learning and LLM research. I am seeking industry roles and Ph.D. opportunities at the intersection of NLP, multimodal learning, and efficient model training.
